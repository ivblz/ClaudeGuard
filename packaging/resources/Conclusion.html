ClaudeGuard is installed

Find the shield icon in the menu bar and choose VPN or Proxy.

VPN: connect to your VPN, open Manage Whitelisted IPs… and add its public IP. Do not whitelist your ordinary connection by mistake.

Proxy: open Configure Proxy… and save a proxy URL or a supported sing-box outbound JSON. Save an empty field to remove a profile.

Existing profiles and whitelist entries are preserved on upgrade. Protection Active controls protection in either mode.

CLI: ~/.local/bin/claudeguard status

Uninstall: ~/.local/bin/claudeguard uninstall
